GTVP

S&D TVET Consult Limited was officially established on March 21, 2024, bringing together a founding team with over 8 years of deep, hands-on expertise in Ghana's TVET sector. We have accredited 22+ institutions, trained 500+ facilitators, and successfully managed major national projects including GTVP and GJS-P.

Our Mission: To position our company as the leading authority for CTVET accreditation, competency-based training, and TVET institutional management across Ghana.

We combine regulatory knowledge with practical experience to help private TVET school proprietors, facilitators, NGOs, and corporate bodies achieve sustainable excellence in technical and vocational training.

Our approach is collaborative, transparent, and results-driven — we don't just advise; we partner with your institution every step of the way.
