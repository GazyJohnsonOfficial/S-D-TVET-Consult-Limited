Who We Are

Building Ghana's TVET Future

S&D TVET Consult Limited is a professional consultancy firm established on 21st March, 2024, headquartered in Lapaz, Greater Accra. We specialize in Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) advisory services across Ghana.

While our company is two years young, our directors bring over 8 years of combined industry expertise — having worked extensively within the TVET ecosystem, CTVET standards frameworks, and competency-based training methodologies.

"Our mission is to raise the quality and accessibility of technical-vocational education in Ghana — empowering institutions, equipping facilitators, and preparing skilled graduates for the workforce."

From policy document development to CTVET accreditation guidance, we provide end-to-end consultancy that transforms TVET institutions into nationally recognized centers of excellence.
